Achieving a 2nd Degree Black Belt in Taekwondo was not just a physical milestone; it was a masterclass in iteration and discipline. 

In many ways, mastering a complex martial arts form (Poomsae) is exactly like writing an algorithm. You start with a series of distinct, isolated movements. At first, they are clunky and disconnected—much like a rough draft of code. But through hundreds of hours of repetition on the mat, you refine the kinetic chain, correct your balance, and optimize your power output until the sequence runs flawlessly. 

Taekwondo taught me that true mastery—whether in compiling a native macOS app or executing a perfect roundhouse kick—doesn't come from a sudden flash of genius. It comes from the quiet, unglamorous discipline of showing up every single day and refusing to quit when things get difficult.
